Theoretical Significance & Bias Analysis

Why Signal Detection Theory is essential in Parapsychology:

  • Debunking the Accuracy Illusion: An untrained experimenter seeing a high Hit rate (e.g. 70%) might falsely claim psychic abilities. SDT reveals that if False Alarms are also ~70%, sensitivity $d'=0$ and the subject merely exhibited a liberal bias ($b > 0$).
  • Double-Blind Protection: By computerizing the trial sequence and isolating the receiver, sensory leakage (Clever Hans effect) is eliminated.
  • Proof of Absence: Using Bayesian $BF_{01} = {{ metrics.bf01|round(2) }}$, we provide affirmative proof that the observer's mind is indistinguishable from random chance.
