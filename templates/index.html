Centre for Neuroscience • Assignment 2

Testing Parapsychology with Signal Detection Theory

Can you prove or disprove ESP using Signal Detection Theory? Traditional tests confuse response bias with perceptual sensitivity. Here we isolate true sensitivity ($d'$) and decision criteria ($c, b$) under rigorous sensory shielding.

Paradigm A • Subject 1 Binary Yes/No

Mental Telepathy Experiment

A Sender focuses on a hidden Zener target during randomized signal intervals. An acoustically & visually isolated Receiver decides whether mental signal transmission occurred.

  • Sensory shielding (Double-blind computer controlled)
  • 50 Randomized trials (50% signal prevalence)
  • Calculates $d'$, criterion $c$, and relative bias $b$
  • Monte Carlo Permutation test & Bayes Factor ($BF_{01}$)
Paradigm B • Subject 2 Confidence Rating ROC

Clairvoyance & ROC Analysis

Tests direct perception of cryptographically concealed targets. The subject uses a 4-point rating scale to map multiple decision criteria and construct an empirical ROC curve.

  • Hardware RNG cryptographic vault shielding
  • 4-level confidence ratings (1: Sure Absent to 4: Sure Present)
  • Constructs empirical ROC curve & calculates empirical AUC
  • Demonstrates that varying criteria traces the $d' \approx 0$ contour

⚡ Load Standardized Benchmark Experiment (n=2 Subjects)

Generates reproducible, scientifically controlled datasets for Alice (Telepathy) and Bob (Clairvoyance ROC) with empirical permutation distributions and statistical proof.
